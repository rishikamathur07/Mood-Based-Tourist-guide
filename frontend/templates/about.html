About Mood

Travel plans that leave room for how you feel.

Mood helps you turn a few choices into a practical day-by-day trip. Pick a destination, your pace, and a spending limit; we organise places, timings, and local travel into one clear plan.

01

Built around your day

Choose a mood such as culture, relaxation, nature, adventure, or nightlife. Your itinerary prioritises places that fit it.

02

Costs you can understand

Entrance tickets, stay, meals, and city transport are shown separately, so you can see what makes up the estimate.

03

Ready when plans change

Save favourites, review completed trips, explore hotels, and refresh a plan when a stop is unavailable or too busy.
